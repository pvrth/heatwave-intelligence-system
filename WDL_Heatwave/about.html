Project context

About the Heatwave Intelligence System

A unified academic prototype for heatwave monitoring, prediction and early warning. The interface merges the mini-project dashboard with the stronger operational concepts from the heatwave-prediction site.

01 · OBJECTIVE

What the system is for

The goal is to convert scattered thermal observations into information that is easier to understand and act on. A user should be able to see current regional conditions, identify hotspots, review a forecast, understand the warning level and access an advisory without moving between unrelated pages.

02 · DATA

What feeds the model

The heatwave concept uses temperature and humidity observations from Automatic Weather Stations, meteorological records associated with IMD, and satellite-derived thermal information. The current website uses illustrative data to demonstrate that workflow.

03 · USERS

Who benefits

Meteorologists need analytical context, disaster-management teams need severity and response cues, and citizens need simple warnings and heat-health actions. The pages are arranged around those different information needs.

Why heatwave intelligence needs more than a temperature number

A single maximum temperature does not explain the full risk picture. Heatwave assessment also depends on how far temperatures depart from the local normal, how long elevated conditions persist, where hotspots are concentrated and how vulnerable the affected population may be.

How the system works

The strongest part carried over from the heatwave-prediction project is its end-to-end operating chain.

01 · DATA ACQUISITION

Collect observations

Gather temperature, humidity and related environmental measurements from stations and other thermal sources.

02 · VALIDATION

Clean and verify

Check for incomplete, inconsistent or outlying observations before they influence a decision.

03 · PREDICTION

Estimate future heat

Use regional and station-level patterns to estimate maximum temperature and persistence.

04 · CLASSIFICATION

Assign severity

Translate values into Normal, Watch, Warning or Severe categories for faster interpretation.

05 · DISSEMINATION

Turn insight into action

Present bulletins, map views, forecast summaries and public advisories in one interface.

System boundaries and assumptions

What this prototype demonstrates

Information architecture, responsive UI, JavaScript-driven filtering and rendering, interactive map behaviour, forms and a consistent alert workflow.

What it does not claim

The figures on this site are demonstration values. The interface is not a replacement for official meteorological bulletins, operational forecasts or government emergency instructions.

“Early and accurate heatwave warnings support faster response and better public awareness.” — project concept statement
