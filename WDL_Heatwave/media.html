The companion project included media links and an awareness layer. This version keeps the useful part — clear public communication and official-resource access — without leaving broken local media players.

AWARENESS BRIEF

Recognise dangerous heat

Heat risk becomes more important when high temperatures persist, nights stay warm or humidity increases perceived heat. The site therefore combines temperature, persistence and warning context instead of relying on a single number.

PUBLIC ACTION

Prepare before the hottest hours

Plan outdoor activity outside peak heat, keep drinking water available, use shade or cooling spaces and check regularly on people who may be more affected by heat.

Suggested awareness message

During periods of extreme heat: reduce unnecessary afternoon exposure, keep yourself hydrated, use light clothing and shade, check on vulnerable people, and follow the latest local public-health and emergency instructions.

Why broken media was removed

The source heatwave package referenced local MP4 and MP3 assets that were not present in the supplied project directory. Rather than shipping dead controls, this merged version turns that area into usable awareness content and links only to resources that are actually reachable from the site.
